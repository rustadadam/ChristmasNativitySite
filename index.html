Bring a creche

What's a creche? A creche (say “kresh”) is a nativity scene: figures of Mary, Joseph, and the baby Jesus at the stable in Bethlehem. Yours can be an heirloom or homemade, tiny or table-sized, and made of anything from olive wood to felt to Legos.
